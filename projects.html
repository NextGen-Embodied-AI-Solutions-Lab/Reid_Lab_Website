Marine autonomy

BlueBoat: autonomous surface vessel

An autonomy stack for the BlueBoat, a small uncrewed surface vessel (USV), covering navigation, obstacle detection, and collision avoidance in coastal and aquaculture waters. It spans the full pipeline from perception, using a stereo camera, lidar, and sonar, through planning and control.

  • Two autonomy stacks, ROS 2 (Nav2) and MOOS-IvP, both driving the vessel through ArduPilot, with a hybrid A* path planner and an MPC trajectory tracker.
  • A custom Nav2 controller that avoids moving vessels while following COLREGs, the international rules for preventing collisions at sea.
  • Simulation in Gazebo with waves, plus a reinforcement learning environment that trains collision avoidance in head-on and crossing encounters.
  • ROS 2
  • Nav2
  • MOOS-IvP
  • ArduPilot
  • Gazebo
  • Reinforcement learning

Agricultural robotics

Amiga: crop-row navigation

Crop-row perception and RTK GPS navigation for the farm-ng Amiga field robot. Two cameras on a gantry look down at the canopy; the perception system finds the crop rows and tells the lab's model predictive controller how far the robot is from the row center, while RTK GPS provides precise global position.

  • Row detection in both the camera view and a stitched bird's-eye view, fused when the two agree. This and the RTK GPS setup have been tested in the field.
  • In progress: learned row segmentation trained on pseudo-labels from Segment Anything (SAM), and stereo depth fusion.
  • A multispectral camera pipeline for plant phenotyping, and an onboard recorder that logs data from every field run.
  • farm-ng Amiga
  • ROS 2
  • OAK-D cameras
  • RTK GPS
  • MPC
  • Segment Anything

Embodied AI and safety

CERL: video understanding for robot missions

A question-answering system for live video from drones, robots, and worksites. A vision-language model describes each scene and flags hazards, such as workers in a trench or a boat striking a buoy. The descriptions are stored in a vector database, so an operator can ask questions in plain language and get an answer along with the matching video clips.

  • Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over video and images, with a Milvus vector database and a web interface built in Gradio.
  • Operators can add their own hazard questions during a mission, and the system periodically rates how well each goal of the mission plan is being met.
  • Next steps: adding radar as an input and testing which hazards the system can reliably detect.
  • Vision-language models
  • RAG
  • Milvus
  • Gradio
