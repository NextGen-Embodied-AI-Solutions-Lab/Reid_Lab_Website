Research

Our work spans the perception and control of individual machines up to how whole agricultural systems use resources. Problems come from growers, operators, and industry, and results are tested on real machines in real conditions.

Agricultural robotics and autonomy

Perception, navigation, and path planning that let agricultural machines work safely and productively in unstructured fields. This line of work began with vision-based tractor guidance in the 1980s and continues with stereo vision, sensor fusion, and autonomous navigation for today's agricultural robots.

Selected work

  • Autonomous navigation and path planning for agricultural robots. Advances in Agri-Food Robotics, 2024
  • Robotics in Agriculture and Forestry. Springer Handbook of Robotics, 2016
  • Stereo vision three-dimensional terrain maps for precision agriculture. Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 2008
  • Vision-Based Guidance of an Agricultural Tractor. IEEE Control Systems Magazine, 1987

Embodied AI beyond the farm

Deploying AI on physical machines in agriculture, construction, and marine applications. These settings share agriculture's hardest problems: unstructured terrain, changing weather, and people working alongside machines.

Selected work

  • Autonomous Driving in Agriculture Leading to Autonomous Worksite Solutions. SAE Technical Papers, 2016
  • R-Gator: An unmanned utility vehicle. Proceedings of SPIE, 2010

Precision and digital agriculture

AI-based decision support and the integration of farm production systems, from in-field crop sensing to the management decisions it informs.

Selected work

  • The Digitalization of Production Agriculture. The Bridge, National Academy of Engineering, 2022
  • Fuzzy logic control of a multispectral imaging sensor for in-field plant sensing. Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 2008

Circular bioeconomy

Methods for measuring and improving how well agricultural and biological systems close their resource loops, in support of the transition toward a circular bioeconomy.

Selected work

  • An Index for Quantifying Circularity of Bioeconomy Systems. Resources, Conservation and Recycling, 2024

Safety of autonomous machinery

Risk assessment and hazard analysis for autonomous agricultural machines: which existing methods apply, where they fall short, and how perception can make machines safer around people and obstacles.

Selected work

  • Identification of Advantages and Limitations of Current Risk Assessment and Hazard Analysis Methods when Applied on Autonomous Agricultural Machineries. Journal of Agricultural Safety and Health, 2024
  • Obstacle detection using stereo vision to enhance safety of autonomous machines. ASAE Annual International Meeting, 2005
